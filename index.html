ABOUT SMART COMMUNITY

A Simple Way to Speak Up About Community Problems

Smart Community Problem Reporting System is a web-based platform that helps residents report local issues in an organized way. Residents can describe a problem, select its category, provide a location, and optionally attach a photograph. They can then check their reports and see status updates.

The goal is to make reporting easier, improve visibility of community concerns, and help administrators coordinate responses. The platform supports reporting and tracking; it does not guarantee that an external authority will resolve every report.

NEED HELP?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to submit a report?

Yes. Create an account and log in to access report submission and your personal dashboard.

What problems can I report?

You can report issues such as damaged roads, waste, water, drainage, street lighting, security and environmental concerns.

Can I track a report after submitting it?

Yes. Log in and open your dashboard or My Reports page to review the status of your reports.

Does submitting a report guarantee a solution?

No. The platform helps record, organize and track reports, but resolution depends on review and action by the responsible parties.
